# Satmola Field Tools

Simple web pages used by the Satmola team.

| Page | Used by | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `index.html` | Owner | Route planning and map view |
| `Location_sender.html` | Owner | Look up a store and share its location |
| `Sales_team.html` | Sales team | Send a store's location and photo |
| `Driver_checkin.html` | Drivers | Check in at a store with GPS and photo |

These pages are for internal company use. Access to company data is restricted.
